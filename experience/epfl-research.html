Robotics Research Intern

Learning Algorithms and Systems Laboratory (LASA), EPFL

Mar. – Aug. 2026 · Lausanne, Switzerland

This internship was through the Excellence Research Internship Program (ERIP) at EPFL, which provides outstanding students from around the world with the opportunity to work in EPFL research labs during the summer. I was selected for this program and worked in the Learning Algorithms and Systems Laboratory (LASA) under the supervision of Prof. Aude Billard. This research internship taught me not only the fundamentals of robotics, but also how to be resilient and adaptable in a new and unfamiliar environment. I really learned a lot and enjoyed so much about working at LASA and living in Switzerland.

The work I have done

  • Developed a force estimation model for a 20-DOF robotic hand using a combination of analytical modeling and machine learning techniques.
  • Implemented system identification methods to characterize the dynamic behavior of the robotic hand, improving control accuracy and responsiveness.
  • Conducted experimental testing to validate the performance of the force estimation model and system identification results, ensuring reliability in real-world applications.
