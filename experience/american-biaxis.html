Co-op

Process Engineering Intern

American Biaxis Inc.

May – Aug. 2024 · Winnipeg, MB

This was my first co-op term, and I learned so much about the manufacturing industry and the professional work environment. American Biaxis is a nylon plastic film manufacturing company in Winnipeg, and I worked in the Process Engineering department.

My work

  • Created a Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for plant operators using a new 6.6-meter-wide, nine-station film slitter, detailing safe work procedures for set-up, loading, and unloading film.
  • Documented an SOP for the Bonfanti automated bridge crane to remove, store, and retrieve 12-ton mill rolls of plastic film, including procedures for executing automatic missions and manual operations.
  • Designed and implemented two Power Automate workflows to replace paper-based communication with SharePoint, ensuring traceable, logged, and effective direction from the Process department to Operations.
  • Developed a JSON event reminder countdown banner on the company SharePoint site, improving visibility and management of upcoming events.
  • Composed a detailed mock recall procedure outlining step-by-step product tracing to ensure a quick and accurate response to customer requests.
  • Conducted tensile, shrinkage, and thickness tests on new biaxially oriented polyamide (BOPA) films, generating detailed analysis reports to support quality assurance and product development.
