Research

Undergraduate Research Assistant

Neuromechanics and Assistive Robotics Lab, University of Waterloo

May – Dec. 2025 · Waterloo, ON

My research involved collecting and preprocessing human gait data and developing a model to predict joint angle trajectories.

The work I have done

  • Collected gait-cycle data from 10 participants using a Vicon motion capture system, IMU and EMG sensors, a treadmill, and Bertec force plates, with treadmill control automated through a MATLAB program.
  • Preprocessed multimodal sensor and marker data through gap filling, filtering, synchronization across devices, coordinate-frame alignment of joint rotation axes, and resolution of sensor disconnection issues.
  • Designed and trained neural network models in Keras and TensorFlow, including Conv2D and BiLSTM layers, to predict one future gait cycle of joint-angle trajectories from synchronized time-series sensor data.
