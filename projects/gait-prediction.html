04 / Neuromechanics research

Gait Trajectory Prediction

May 2025 – Dec. 2025

Undergraduate Research Assistant (URA) at University of Waterloo’s Neuromechanics and Assistive Robotics Lab (Arami lab) for S25 and F25 terms. Collected and preprocessed gait data from 10 participants using Vicon motion capture, IMUs, EMG sensors, and force plates. Designed and trained a hybrid Conv2D-BiLSTM model to predict joint angle trajectories one gait cycle ahead.

  • Keras / TensorFlow
  • Time-series modeling
  • Motion capture

Part of my Undergraduate Research Assistant role at Neuromechanics and Assistive Robotics Lab, University of Waterloo.
