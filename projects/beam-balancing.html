Beam Balancing Control System

Sept. 2026 – Oct. 2026

A beam balancing system using PID control, combining mechanical design, electronics, and embedded programming to maintain balance.

  • SolidWorks
  • MATLAB
  • Arduino
  • Raspberry Pi
  • Designed a servo-actuated beam balancer in SolidWorks, machined and assembled mechanical components, soldered a custom PCB, and integrated Arduino, Raspberry Pi, phototransistors, and multiplexer circuitry.
  • Developed and tuned a PID feedback controller using webcam and phototransistor inputs to stabilize ball position.
